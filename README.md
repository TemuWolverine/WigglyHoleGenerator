# Wiggly Hole Generator - Fusion 360 Add-In

**Wiggly Hole Generator** is a  Autodesk Fusion 360 add-in designed for  weirdos like me - I guess mechanical engineers, product designers, and 3D printing enthusiasts. 

It automates the generation of internal **Compliant Flexural Fins**, **Coathanger Flexures** (I don't know what they're actually called), and **Crush Ribs** inside cylindrical holes for press-fit pins, dowels, bearings, and fasteners.

## How to Install & Run

The add-in is linked to your Fusion 360 Add-Ins directory:
`%APPDATA%\Autodesk\Autodesk Fusion 360\API\AddIns\WigglyHoleGenerator`

### In Fusion 360:
1. Open **Autodesk Fusion 360**.
2. Go to **Utilities** tab > **Add-Ins** > **Scripts and Add-Ins** (shortcut: `Shift + S`).
3. Select the **Add-Ins** tab.
4. Locate **WigglyHoleGenerator** in the list.
5. Check **Run on Startup** if you want it to launch automatically every time Fusion starts.
6. Click **Run**.
7. Navigate to the **Design** workspace > **Solid** tab > **Create** panel.
8. Click the **Wiggly Hole Generator** button (located on the toolbar).
9. Select a hole, tweak the properties, and click **OK**!
